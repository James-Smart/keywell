How the app recognises people

{{- template "admin-field-error" .Errors.For "subject_mode"}}
  • Keywell ID: A permanent ID that never changes, even if their email does.
  • Email address: Changes when their email changes.
  • Username: People without a username get their Keywell ID.

Changing this changes how the app recognises people, so existing accounts in the app may stop matching.
